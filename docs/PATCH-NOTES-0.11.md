# Mod the Show Manager 0.11

- **Code mods switch on properly now**, and you can flip them while you play: F2 steps through them, F3 turns
  the one shown on or off. Auto PCI can also show the PCI moving onto the pitch.
- **Discover:** a new tab for mods other people have shared, and league packs you can pass around.
- **Scorebug builder:** make a scorebug of your own with live game data, team colours that follow the uniform,
  your own art and fonts, and a set of ready-made designs to start from. It works on all four themes.
- **Stadium boards and broadcast screens** open in the same builder and can be matched to your scorebug, with a
  lineup card and an inning break card to go with it.
- **Packages:** save a whole look as one mod and share it.
- **Creator tools:** a jersey builder, higher resolution textures, click-to-texture in stadiums and random
  players.
- General improvements and fixes across the app.

The builder is new, so try your design in a game before you share it. Rebuild the game once in Code Plugins after
updating. Keep the logs coming (Settings › Open Log).
